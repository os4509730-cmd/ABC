⚡ High-Probability Candlestick Cheat Sheet

🔨 Hammer BULLISH REVERSAL

Forms after a downtrend. Long lower wick (2x body) proves buyers defended the support floor.

STOP-LOSS: 1-2 ticks beneath lowest wick tip.

☄️ Shooting Star BEARISH REVERSAL

Forms at resistance peak. Long upper shadow proves bulls were repelled and ambushed.

STOP-LOSS: Right above peak of upper wick.

🛡️ Bullish Engulfing MOMENTUM SHIFT

Green body completely engulfs prior red body. Complete buyer takeover of supply.

STOP-LOSS: Below the low of the green engulfing bar.

🩸 Bearish Engulfing TITAN BREACH

Crushing red candle swallows previous green candle. Sellers seize total control.

STOP-LOSS: Just above the high of the red engulfing bar.

⚖️ Long-Legged Doji EQUILIBRIUM / INDECISION

Open and close are identical. Extreme battle range but zero net advance. Wait for confirmation.

EXECUTION: Trade the breakout above or below extreme wick.

⚔️ Marubozu PURE CONVICTION

Zero wicks. Absolute dominance from the opening bell to the close. Strong breakout fuel.

STOP-LOSS: Halfway point of the Marubozu body.
